# Contest rules and submission requirements

Reviewed: September 26, 2026 (Asia/Kolkata). Source: the owner's uploaded seven-page "Handshake AI Skills Studio X OpenAI Multiplayer Game Challenge Official Rules" PDF. Page references below are PDF page numbers.

This is a project summary. The official rules govern; recheck them before entering.

## Entry deadline and submission

- Entry period: September 22, 2026 through October 30, 2026, 11:59 PM Pacific.
- India equivalent of the deadline: October 31, 2026, 12:29 PM IST. Pacific is on daylight time on that date.
- Submit through the Handshake AI Skills Studio "Create a Multiplayer Game" mission.
- Required entry materials: project title, cover image, description, and project URL.
- A GitHub repository or public game URL alone does not complete the contest entry.
- Multiple entries are permitted. Mass entries and script/macro/automated-device entries are prohibited.
- Entry is free; paying does not increase the chance of winning.

Source: pages 1-2 and 4. Recommended internal submission target: October 29, 2026; this is a planning target, not an organizer requirement.

## Eligibility

- Entrants must be individuals aged at least 18 or the applicable age of majority.
- They must be legal residents of an eligible country and physically present in an eligible country when entering.
- India appears in Appendix A.
- An active Handshake account must be associated with the entry email.
- Exclusions include Handshake officers, directors, employees, their immediate families and household members, promotional partners, advertising/promotion agencies, and prize suppliers.

Source: page 1 and Appendix A, pages 5-7.

The rules do not explicitly clarify the status of independent AI-project contributors/contractors. If an entrant's relationship to Handshake could fall within an exclusion, the organizer should clarify eligibility before entry. This unresolved interpretation does not by itself establish either eligibility or ineligibility and is not a blocker to building the game.

## Judging

Each criterion is scored on a 1-5 scale with equal weight.

| Criterion | Weight | What a strong project needs | Our planned evidence, not yet achieved |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Execution | 25% | Complete requirements, stable end-to-end operation, and a working demonstration | Separate-session multiplayer tests and public URL verification |
| Creativity | 25% | A fresh concept, distinctive idea, or clever implementation | A clearly explained game mechanic and meaningful player cooperation |
| Usefulness / Value | 25% | An enjoyable experience that serves its intended audience | Real-player feedback, replay value, and participation for every player |
| Polish & Thoughtfulness | 25% | Clear guidance, usable design, and considered edge cases | Phone/desktop checks, onboarding, reconnection, and useful error states |

The top 20 entries advance to a second round judged by Handshake or sponsor judges using the same criteria. Source: pages 2-3.

These weights mean visuals alone cannot compensate for unreliable multiplayer, and feature count alone does not establish quality. This is our design interpretation, not an additional rule.

## Prizes and entry consequences

- The top three winners receive USD 1,000 each.
- Notifications go to the email associated with the Handshake account within 30 days after the deadline.
- Winners may need to provide eligibility proof and acceptance/release paperwork.
- Winners are responsible for applicable taxes and costs.
- The organizer reserves the right to cancel the contest or not award a prize.
- Entry grants broad, continuing promotional-use permissions for the submission and specified entrant information. Read the "Information Submitted" section before entering.

Source: pages 1-3.
